## New

**Google Maps Leads Scraper: Category Match by an AI classifier**

For a search and each Google Maps category, a classifier answers how likely a business listed under that category is one you are looking for. A place's `category_match_score` is its best category's; a place named with the searched words, whole and in order, scores 1 (a brand, a chain). Category Match keeps the places at 0.5 or more. See [Google Maps Leads Scraper](/examples/google-maps-leads-scraper/update-settings).

**Google Maps Leads Scraper: whole-area tasks are estimated before the run**

A task over a whole area (a search and an area) gets its own estimate, read from the search's count in the country and the area's share of its places, instead of the default 100 rows. A search that names a brand is counted by its name, and an estimate is never below 5 places.

## Improved

**Google Maps Leads Scraper: 43 more Search Regions, and every region searches its own country**

15 regions were spelled differently in the list and in the engine ("Türkiye", "Côte d'Ivoire", "Bosnia & Herzegovina"...) and searched Google as the United States. Each now searches its own country, and 43 regions Google serves are added: Monaco, Réunion, Martinique, Guadeloupe, New Caledonia, Bermuda...

**Google Maps Leads Scraper: 6 more Search Languages**

English (United Kingdom), English (Australia), Français (Canada), Chinese (Hong Kong), Serbian (Latin) and Belarusian: 80 of the languages Google Maps serves.

**Google Maps Leads Scraper: categories matched as Google names them**

A category from Google's list is matched word for word, British or American spelling alike (Hardware shop and Hardware store, Petrol station and Gas station). A listed category almost no place carries ("Moving company", where Google files movers as "Mover") is compared by meaning, as before.

**Google Maps Leads Scraper: small dense areas run in parallel**

An area of a few km² started from one window, searched one after the other. It now starts from up to 16 windows searched at once: the City of London, Shibuya, Monaco, Le Marais. An area across 180 degrees (the United States with the Aleutians, Russia, New Zealand) starts on its own land, where it returned 0 results.

**Google Maps results: one row per business on a run without emails**

A run without email extraction could return a business twice or more, once per email other runs had found for it. It now returns one row per business. A run with emails keeps one row per email.

**Google Maps emails: contact form placeholders left out**

Addresses like `jane@email.com` or `jean.dupont@entreprise.fr`, the examples written in contact forms, are no longer returned as a business's email.

**LinkedIn Posts Commenters and Likers: share links and hidden posts**

A share link (`lnkd.in/...`) is followed to its post, where it returned 0 rows; a URL with no post ends `invalid_url`. A post your account cannot see ends `access_restricted` instead of reading as a post with no comment. See [LinkedIn Posts Commenters and Likers Scraper](/examples/linkedin-posts-commenters-and-likers-scraper/add-tasks).

## Fixed

**LinkedIn Posts Scraper: Fetch Since on an account in French**

A LinkedIn account in French shows a post's age as "3 mois", "2 sem", "5 j": `posted_at` stayed empty and Fetch Since kept every post. French ages are now read. See [LinkedIn Posts Scraper](/examples/linkedin-posts-scraper/update-settings).

**File uploads to Google Maps Leads Scraper**

Every CSV or TSV sent to Google Maps Leads Scraper failed with "something is wrong", in the app and through [Upload Tasks](/docs/upload-tasks). Uploads work again.

**Capterra Reviews Scraper: products with more than one page of reviews**

Every product past its first page of reviews failed. Its reviews come back, page after page.

**Google News: articles with very long URLs**

An article whose URL ran past 2,000 characters stopped the run. It is kept without its query string, which only named the site's experiments.

**Contact Details Scraper: phones with hidden characters**

A phone written with invisible characters on a site failed the result. They are now removed and the phone kept.

**A run starting is not launched twice**

A run could be started again in the 20 seconds before it said it was running, and two workers could take the same task. It now starts once.
