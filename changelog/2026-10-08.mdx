## New

**Reddit Scraper — Sort Period**

- `sort_period` sets the time window of a ranking: `all` (default), `year`, `month`, `week`, `day` or `hour`. It applies to subreddit feeds sorted by `top` or `controversial`, and to searches sorted by `relevance`, `top` or `comments`. A `t` already in the task URL (e.g. `https://www.reddit.com/r/Bitcoin/top/?t=year`) wins.
- Squids already set to `top` or `controversial` now rank over all time. Reddit's own default for these feeds is the last 24 hours, which is what they returned until now: set `sort_period` to `day` to keep that.

See [update-settings](/examples/reddit-scraper/update-settings).

**Facebook Marketplace Scraper — Newer than and Exclude Results Outside Your Search**

- `fetch_since` (Newer than) keeps only listings published after a point in time: a relative duration (`"24h"`, `"7d"`, `"2w"`) or a date (`YYYY-MM-DD`, read in `fetch_since_timezone` when set). On search URLs, Facebook's matching Date listed filter (last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days) is added for you, and the older listings Facebook still returns are skipped one by one and not billed. Set it to your schedule interval to collect, and pay for, only the new listings of each run.
- `exclude_outside_search` (off by default) skips the listings Facebook shows under "Results from outside your search" (outside your search area, or only loosely related to your query) and stops paging once that section starts. Skipped listings are not billed.
- New result field `is_outside_search`: `true` for the listings under that section, `false` before it, empty on browse and category URLs.

See [update-settings](/examples/facebook-marketplace-scraper/update-settings) and [get-results](/examples/facebook-marketplace-scraper/get-results).

## Improved

**Reddit Scraper — feed limits spelled out**

Reddit serves about 1,000 posts per feed at most (new, top, controversial, search), so on a busy subreddit `new` only reaches back a few weeks. The [update-settings](/examples/reddit-scraper/update-settings) page now explains how to go further back (several sorts and periods, plus keyword searches scoped to the subreddit), and that `fetch_since` ends the task at the first older post, so it belongs with `sort_subreddit` set to `new`.

**Facebook Marketplace Scraper — Get Listing Details cost spelled out**

Get Listing Details is billed once per squid: on later runs, the listings this squid already enriched are skipped and not billed again. This was already the behaviour; the [credit cost](/examples/facebook-marketplace-scraper/credit-cost) page now says so.

**All scrapers — a blocked or down website pauses the run instead of ending it in error**

When the website being scraped refused our requests (HTTP 403 or 429) or did not answer properly (HTTP 5xx) even after retries, the run used to end as `ERROR`. It now pauses for 10 minutes with `done_reason` `website_inaccessible` and resumes on its own, picking up only the unfinished tasks, up to 3 times per run; a fourth time it ends as `ERROR` as before. Runs you poll or receive by webhook will show `PAUSED` then `RUNNING` again instead of `ERROR`. See [errors](/docs/errors).

## Fixed

**Pinterest Scraper — pins with an encoded id**

Pinterest now gives some pins an encoded id such as `e1_AfqPShzqO1C7_LEkrAbtoIWUe2MoGdDTj5vU32Lr9gXn25qs1FpIvg` instead of a number. These pins made the run stop with an error; they are now collected like any other pin. `pin_id` stays a string: store it as text, not as a number. See [get-results](/examples/pinterest-scraper/get-results).
