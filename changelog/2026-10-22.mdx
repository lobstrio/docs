## Improved

**Reddit Scraper: every form of a post URL, and the subscribers again**

A post URL without its slug (`/r/<sub>/comments/<id>/`), without its trailing slash or without its subreddit (`/comments/<id>/`) now returns the post and its comments, where it ended with 0 rows or was refused. Since Reddit stopped serving its `.json` pages to visitors, post details come from Reddit's own API: `SUBREDDIT_SUBSCRIBERS` is filled again, and runs no longer take hours. A feed gives about 500 posts sorted by hot, top or controversial, and about 1,000 by new. See [Reddit Scraper](/examples/reddit-scraper/add-tasks).

**Pages Jaunes: department and region URLs**

`/annuaire/departement/isere-38/electricien` and `/annuaire/region/...` read the department or the region as the place, where they returned 0 results. A listing Pages Jaunes itself cannot serve is kept with its search data, and the run goes on instead of pausing. See [Pages Jaunes](/examples/pages-jaunes-iter-companies/add-tasks).

**Cards are added through Stripe**

A card is now added with Stripe's own form, 3-D Secure included: its number never reaches lobstr.io. An account adds at most 10 cards an hour.

## Fixed

**Google Maps Reviews Scraper: edited reviews flagged again**

Reviews now come from Google Search, where the edit date is not given: every review read as not edited. `IS_MODIFIED` now follows the review's own label (Edited, Modifié, Bearbeitet, Editado...). And "Newer than" is compared before the rating and text filters, so a task with filters stops at the first older review instead of turning page after page. See [Google Maps Reviews Scraper](/examples/google-maps-reviews-scraper/get-results).

**Instagram Reels Scraper: business accounts**

Instagram refuses its profile API for some business accounts, and their reels were skipped as restricted. The account is now read from its public page, and its reels come back. See [Instagram Reels Scraper](/examples/instagram-reels-scraper/add-tasks).

**Google Maps Leads Scraper: a place's posts never stop the run**

A network error while reading a place's posts is tried again, then the posts are skipped. It paused the run, and could end it in error.

**Facebook Business Page Scraper: long profile picture URLs**

Facebook's profile picture URLs now run past 500 characters, and saving them failed the task. They are kept whole, up to 2,000 characters.

**Crunchbase Companies Scraper: a page answering 404 while the session warms up**

The task failed in error; the next page is tried instead.

**Email verification resumes after a restart on our side**

A verification interrupted by a restart of our verification service stayed in progress forever, and the run had no results file. It now resumes.

**Deleting a squid frees its slots at once**

A deleted squid's run kept its place on our servers for 2 hours, and other runs waited for a worker.
