---
title: "Credit Cost"
description: "Credit cost for the Similarweb Scraper"
---

| Function | Cost (credits) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export Websites | 1 | 1 credit per website. |
| Company Details | 1 | 1 extra credit per website when `company_details` is enabled. |
| AI Traffic | 1 | 1 extra credit per website when `ai_traffic` is enabled. |
| Competitor Details | 1 | 1 extra credit per website when `competitor_details` is enabled. |
