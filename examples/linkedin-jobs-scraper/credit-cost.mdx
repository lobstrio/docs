---
title: "Credit Cost"
description: "Credit cost for the LinkedIn Jobs Scraper"
---

| Function | Cost (credits) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export Jobs | 1 | 1 credit per job posting. |
| Full Job Details | 0 | Included — on by default, no extra credits. |
| Company Details | 1 | 1 extra credit per job posting when `company_details` is enabled. Off by default. |
