---
title: "Credit Cost"
description: "Credit cost for the LinkedIn Jobs Scraper"
---

| Function | Cost (credits) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export Jobs | 1 | 1 credit per job posting (title, company, location, posted date, apply link, snippet). |
| Full Job Details | 1 | 1 extra credit per job when `job_details` is enabled. Off by default. Free when the task input is a single job URL, since that posting page is already scraped in full. |
| Company Details | 1 | 1 extra credit per job when `company_details` is enabled. Off by default. |
