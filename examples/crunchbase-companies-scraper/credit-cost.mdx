---
title: "Credit Cost"
description: "Credit cost for the Crunchbase Companies Scraper"
---

| Function | Cost (credits) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export Companies | 1 | 1 credit per company. |
| Full Company Details | 1 | 1 extra credit per company when `company_details` is enabled on a search. Company URLs return the full details for free on the first request. |

<Note>
  Logged out, Crunchbase serves at most 15 companies per search page and refuses to paginate. Use the search filters to slice a market into as many 15-company windows as you need, or feed company URLs (no such limit).
</Note>
