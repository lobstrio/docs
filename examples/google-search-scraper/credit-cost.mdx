---
title: "Credit Cost"
description: "Credit cost for the Google Search Scraper"
---

| Function | Cost (credits) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export Results | 1 | 1 credit per organic result row. |
| Collect Shopping Results | 1 | 1 extra credit per shopping result (first page, up to 40 products) when `collect_shopping_results` is enabled. Off by default. |
