---
title: "Credit Cost"
description: "Credit cost for the Google Play Store Scraper"
---

| Function | Cost (credits) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export Google Play Results | 1 | 1 credit per row. App rows and review rows cost the same. |

<Note>
  Turn off `collect_reviews` to collect app listings only (one row per app). With `collect_reviews` on (the default), a popular app can add thousands of review rows.
</Note>
