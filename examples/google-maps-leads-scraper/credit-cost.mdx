description: "Credit cost for the Google Maps Leads Scraper"

| Function | Cost (credits) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export Local Businesses | 1 | 1 credit per row. |
| Extract Emails from Website | 1 | 1 credit per row if any email address is found. No email found = 0 credits. |
| Verify Emails | 2 | 2 credits per email if the status is valid or unknown. Invalid = 0 credits. |
| Collect Business Details | 1 | 1 credit per row. |
| Fetch Business Images | 1 | 1 credit per local business, including up to 250 images. |
