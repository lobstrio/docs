---
title: "Credit Cost"
description: "Credit cost for the LinkedIn Search Scraper"
---

| Function | Cost (credits) | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Export LinkedIn Search Results | 1 | 1 credit per row (person, company or post). |
| Profile Details | 1 | 1 extra credit per profile when `profile_details` is enabled on a people search. |
| Company Details | 1 | 1 extra credit per company when `company_details` is enabled on a company search. |
| Post Engagement | 1 | 1 extra credit per post when `post_engagement` is enabled on a post search. |

<Note>
  LinkedIn caps every search at about 1,000 results. Use filters or several searches to slice a bigger query.
</Note>
