---

lobstr.io runs on a credit system: every plan includes a monthly credit allowance, and each scraper run consumes credits based on the results and enrichment functions it uses. See [Get Balance](/docs/get-balance) to check your current credits and [Get Run Credits](/docs/get-run-credits) to see exactly how a specific run's credits were spent. Each scraper's own Credit Cost page documents what it charges, e.g. [Google Maps Leads Scraper](/examples/google-maps-leads-scraper/credit-cost).

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly price | Annual price (billed yearly, 20% off) | Credits / month | Concurrent slots | Data retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | — | 100 | 1 | 7 days |
| Starter | $20 | $192 | 10,000 | 2 | 28 days |
| Pro | $100 | $960 | 100,000 | 10 | 28 days |
| Team | $500 | $4,800 | 1,000,000 | 50 | 28 days |

<Note>
  Need more than the Team plan offers? Custom/Enterprise pricing is available — contact us.
</Note>

See the full comparison and try the [pricing simulator](https://www.lobstr.io/pricing#simulator) to estimate credit usage for your use case.
